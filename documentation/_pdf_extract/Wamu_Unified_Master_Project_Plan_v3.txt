1. Executive Decision
The revised Wamu project plan and the broader Wamu master vision can be combined. The recommended product strategy is not to discard either document, but to separate the product into a transaction core and relationship/discovery layers.
The transaction-first plan should remain the execution backbone because it gives Wamu a measurable reason to exist from the first production release: Discover → Chat → Buy → Pay → Deliver → Review.
The social/chat/community vision should remain the long-term network layer. It should be introduced progressively, beginning with transactional business chat and expanding into general relationships and communities once the commerce loop is stable.
This creates one coherent product: Wamu is a social-economic network whose core engine is trusted local commerce.

3. Product Vision
Wamu is an Uganda-focused social-commerce and economic network connecting people, communities, businesses, products, payments, delivery and mobility.
The long-term journey is:
Connect → Communicate → Discover → Trust → Transact → Deliver → Repeat → Grow
The first production loop is intentionally smaller:
Discover → Chat → Buy → Pay → Deliver → Review

5. The Critical Change: Chat
The project plan is correct to avoid launching a WhatsApp clone. However, chat should not be removed from the product vision. It should be split into two capabilities.
5.1 Transactional chat — early
Customer ↔ merchant chat.
Order-linked conversations.
Product cards shared inside chat.
Delivery status updates in the order conversation.
Customer support conversations.
Receipts/order references inside relevant conversations.
5.2 Social chat — later
Friend-to-friend messaging.
Family and friend groups.
Communities.
Voice notes and richer media.
Status/stories.
Social sharing between relationships.
This resolves the apparent conflict: Wamu does not need general chat to prove its first value proposition, but chat remains a strategic pillar for the eventual social-economic network.

7. Kampala Beachhead
Start in Kampala with food and groceries because they provide frequent purchases, clear delivery needs, relatively understandable catalogs and measurable repeat behavior.
Do not interpret this as a permanent restriction. It is a controlled learning environment.
Phase 0: 10 merchants, approximately 20 riders, manual dispatch.
Phase 1: production commerce and delivery MVP.
Prove repeat orders, payment success, delivery reliability and merchant retention.
Expand categories only when the operational loop is stable.
Then introduce broader social and discovery features.

9. Core Economic Flywheel
The combined strategy creates two connected loops.
Commerce loop: Discover → Chat → Buy → Pay → Deliver → Review → Repeat
Social loop: Connect → Chat → Community → Share → Discover → Transact
The loops reinforce each other. Social relationships create discovery and referrals; commerce creates useful reasons to return; business growth attracts more businesses and products.

18. Critical State Machines
18.1 Order
PENDING → CONFIRMED → PROCESSING → READY → OUT_FOR_DELIVERY → DELIVERED
Cancellation: PENDING/CONFIRMED → CANCELLED. Refunds must be handled through explicit backend-controlled states and payment reconciliation.
18.2 Delivery
REQUESTED → RIDER_ASSIGNED → ACCEPTED → GOING_TO_PICKUP → PRODUCT_COLLECTED → IN_TRANSIT → DELIVERED
18.3 Rider verification
DRAFT → SUBMITTED → UNDER_REVIEW → APPROVED/REJECTED → RESUBMIT or SUSPENDED/EXPIRED
Clients must never be trusted to declare payments successful, orders delivered or verification approved.
19. Security, Privacy & Compliance
Never store raw OTPs or log authentication secrets.
Hash refresh tokens and secure sessions.
Use strict authorization and role-based access.
Keep verification documents private and access-controlled.
Encrypt sensitive information appropriately.
Use idempotency for payments and critical order operations.
Verify payment webhooks cryptographically where supported.
Maintain audit logs.
Apply rate limiting and anti-abuse controls.
Validate uploaded media.
Minimize retained location history.
Implement block, report, moderation and dispute workflows.
Review Uganda's data protection, payments, consumer, tax, communications and transport requirements before launching regulated capabilities.

26. Final Recommendation
The uploaded revised project should not be discarded. It should become the execution core of this unified Wamu plan.
The strongest reconciliation is:
Keep transaction-first MVP.
Keep business/order chat in the MVP because it directly supports transactions.
Delay general social chat until the commerce loop is proven.
Keep communities as a later retention/network layer.
Keep short video as a later commerce-focused discovery engine.
Keep the rider network for delivery first and passenger mobility later.
Keep the existing FastAPI/PostgreSQL/Redis/modular-monolith architecture.
Use business data and transaction history to introduce advanced analytics and AI later.
Expand into B2B and regional commerce only after domestic operations are reliable.
This gives Wamu a coherent identity: not a collection of copied apps, but a platform where social relationships and local economic activity reinforce one another.
27. One-Sentence Product Definition
Wamu is a social-economic network that helps people connect and communicate, discover local businesses, transact safely, receive goods and services, and helps Ugandan enterprises grow.
