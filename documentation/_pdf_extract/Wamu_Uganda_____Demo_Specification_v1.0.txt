     Wamu Uganda — Demo Specification v1.0
     Purpose: A buildable, screen-by-screen specification for the fundable demo.
     Audience: Solo developer building the demo in 6–10 weeks.
     Philosophy: The demo must feel like nothing that exists in Uganda today. Not Jumia. Not WhatsApp.
     Not TikTok. Not SafeBoda. All four, in one app, with a shared soul.
     Version: 1.0
     Date: 2026-09-27




     1. Demo Purpose & Success Criteria

     1.1 What the Demo Must Prove (in 10 minutes)
       1. Discovery is social — short video feed of local businesses, not a product grid.
       2. Commerce is conversational — chat is the store, not a cart.
       3. Payment is local — MTN MoMo and Airtel Money, mocked but shown end to end.
       4. Delivery is personal — a named, verified rider with a face, rating, and history.
       5. The rider network is shared — the same rider does delivery and rides.

       6. One account, many roles — a customer can become a rider, a merchant can become a
          customer.


     1.2 Success Criteria

       Criterion             Target

       Demo length           10 minutes live, 3 minutes video

       Screens shown         14–18 screens across 3 apps

       "Wow" moments         At least 3 (video feed, chat ordering, rider switch)

       Investor reaction     "This is not Jumia"

       Technical stability   No crashes during a 10-minute walkthrough

     2. The Demo Story (Narrative)
     The demo follows one customer, one merchant, and one rider through a complete loop. The
     presenter narrates.


     Characters (Seeded)

       Role                  Name         Details

       Customer              Sarah        28, works in Ntinda, orders lunch daily

       Merchant              Mama Grace   Food stall in Kamwokya, sells fresh tilapia and matoke

       Rider                 Ronald       32, boda rider, 340 deliveries, 120 rides, 4.8 rating


     The Story
         Sarah opens Wamu at lunch. She scrolls the video feed and sees Mama Grace cooking fresh
         tilapia today. She taps the video, opens a chat, asks if tilapia is available, negotiates a price,
         and places an order. She pays with MTN MoMo. Ronald, a verified rider she has used before,
         picks up the order and delivers it. Sarah rates both. Then Sarah needs to go to town. She
         opens Wamu Move. Ronald is still available. She requests a ride. Same rider, same app,
         same account.

     Every screen in the demo serves this story.

     10. What Comes After the Demo
     If funding is secured:

       1. Replace mocked payments with real MTN MoMo and Airtel Money integrations.
       2. Add real OTP via SMS provider.
       3. Add push notifications via FCM.
       4. Harden security (encryption, audit logs, rate limiting).
       5. Build merchant settlement and rider payouts.
       6. Add admin tools for verification, disputes, refunds.

       7. Launch in one Kampala neighborhood with 20 merchants, 50 riders.
       8. Measure the loop — orders/day, repeat rate, delivery time.
       9. Expand to more neighborhoods once the loop is stable.
