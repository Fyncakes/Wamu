     1. Executive Summary
     Wamu is Uganda’s everyday app: one account for communication, local business discovery,
     commerce, delivery, and mobility. The original vision was correct, but the execution sequence was
     wrong. Building messaging first would compete directly with WhatsApp without offering a
     compelling reason to switch.

     This revised plan flips the strategy. Wamu will build the transaction loop first:

         Discover → Chat → Buy → Pay → Deliver → Review

     The first production MVP will prove one complete, repeatable value loop: a customer orders from a
     merchant, pays by mobile money or cash, and a verified rider delivers it. Messaging, short video,
     and passenger mobility follow only after this loop is stable and repeatable.

     The goal is not to clone WhatsApp, TikTok, or SafeBoda. The goal is to make them obsolete inside
     one connected ecosystem by making every interaction transactional.

     4. The Core Value Loop
         Discover → Chat → Buy → Pay → Deliver → Review

     This is the heart of Wamu. Everything else is an extension.

       1. Discover: Customer finds a local business or product.
       2. Chat: Customer talks to the business or places an order directly.

       3. Buy: Customer selects products or services.
       4. Pay: Customer pays via MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, or cash.

       5. Deliver: A verified rider picks up and delivers the order.
       6. Review: Customer rates the business and rider.

     Once this loop works repeatedly, Wamu adds:

           Transactional chat

           Short business video discovery
           Passenger mobility

           Merchant settlement and payouts
           Intelligence and personalization

     10. Security, Privacy & Compliance

     10.1 Security Controls
           Never store raw OTPs.
           Hash refresh tokens.

           Never log OTPs or tokens.
           Private encrypted storage for identity documents.

           Strict authorization for verification data.
           Audit logs for sensitive access.
           Rate limiting by phone, IP, and device.

           File size/type/content validation.
           Server-side authorization.

           Fake-account and duplicate-identity controls.
           GPS manipulation detection.
           Fake-trip and payment-fraud controls.

           Delivery dispute workflows.
           Fake-review monitoring.

           Suspicious-device-change monitoring.


     10.2 Privacy
           Detailed location history should not be retained forever.
           Active-trip location should be available only for the relevant operational and safety period.

           Government ID images and sensitive verification records must never be publicly displayed.
           Customers see a safe verified profile: name, photo, verified badge, vehicle type, plate, rating,
           trip count, Wamu rider reference.


     10.3 Compliance

     18. Conclusion
     Wamu’s ideal is correct: one app for chat, discovery, commerce, delivery, and mobility. But the
     execution must be transaction-first.

     Build the loop:

         Discover → Chat → Buy → Pay → Deliver → Review

     Then add chat.
     Then add video.
     Then add rides.
     Then add everything.

     That is how Wamu gets users off WhatsApp, TikTok, and SafeBoda.
     Not by cloning them.
     By making them obsolete inside one connected ecosystem.

     Build the loop, not the features.
