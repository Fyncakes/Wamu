WAMU Uganda Super App
MVP Engineering Specification
Working name: WAMU
Tagline: Connect. Discover. Do More.
Initial market: Uganda
Primary platform: Android
Backend: Python + FastAPI
Database: PostgreSQL
Mobile: Flutter




1. MVP OBJECTIVE
The first WAMU release should prove one important hypothesis:


       Can Ugandan users discover local businesses, communicate with them, purchase
       products/services and eventually complete transactions from one application?


The MVP should therefore focus on:


    1. User accounts
    2. Business accounts
    3. Business discovery
    4. Product/service listings
    5. Search
    6. Messaging
    7. Orders
    8. Notifications
    9. Payment-provider integration layer
   10. Administration

Do not build video, ride-hailing, maps, news and a full social network in version 1.

11. PAYMENT ARCHITECTURE
Do not make the WAMU application directly hold customer funds initially.


Create an abstraction:



  class PaymentProvider:
      def initiate_payment(self, amount, currency, reference):
          ...

       def check_status(self, reference):
           ...




                                                    8
       def refund(self, reference):
           ...


Then providers can implement:



  MTN
  Airtel
  Card provider
  Other licensed providers


Architecture:



  WAMU
   │
   ▼
  Payment Service
   │
   ├── MTN Adapter
   ├── Airtel Adapter
   └── Card Adapter


This means changing providers later doesn't require rewriting the entire application.


Payment webhooks must be verified, idempotent and logged.
